Problem Statement - To develop a Multi Linear Regression Model predicting the Service Pack Price 

The Target variable is the price column in service_pack_quote file
Guid connects both printer_data and service_pack_qoute 

Approach 

- Loading both the datasets and explored the data with 
df.head(),df.isnull().sum,df.info(),df.describe()

Findings :

-printDPI and listPriceCurrent were strings 
- Boolean columns were - hasA3,hasA4,hascolor,scan,fax

- For service_pack_quote : 0 nulls in printer data file and no duplicate guid too
                         32 null values in Price column of service_quote 
                         Out of 541 rows only 510 unique guids 
                         12 duplicate rows
                         45 device product numbers contain # character




Data Cleaning :

- cleaned price by removing whitespace,$ and commas, converted it to numeric 
removed whitespace fro  all string columns in printer data 
- split the printDPI string , it had uppercase and lowercase both and had space in between and also few cases where it was '0' too 
- Merged on guid resulting in 415 rows 


Calculated device type statistics like mean,median,mode for both device price and  service pack price. Mean > Median indicating right skewed distribution 

-- MFD device cost much more than single function devices 


-- Handling Data & Analysis

--listpricecurrent had 15 NA imputed with median,price had 27 NA that also imputed with median 
-- Outliers observed considering -1.5 IQR to +1.5 IQR range 
-- Zero negative price column
-- HP was dominating 
-- Calculated correlations and found pairs > 0.5


-- Feature Engineering 

One hot encoding done for make and devicetype and also avoid dummy variable trap 
converted Booleans to 0 and 1 
dropped guid and device_product_number as they dont contribute to prediction 

-- Training & Testing 

70% train and 30 % test,random state = 42 

maximuminputcapacity, listpricecurrent showed very high correlation 

Training and Testing R^2 indicating no overfitting 

features like hasa3,HP printer,hascolor contribute most in predicting service pack price 

Actual vs Predicted plot shows heteroscedasticity means model is less accuracte for higher service packs
